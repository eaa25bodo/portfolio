BLÅ SOL

Gamifying sustainable behaviour at festivals

Blå Sol Festival is rooted in Randers and built on local initiative, volunteering and collaboration. The festival was created with the aim of bringing people together around music and cultural experiences in East Jutland.

ClientBlå Sol Festival
RoleUX Research, UI/UX Design, Web development
Duration4 weeks
DateMay 2026
ToolsFigma, FigJam, VS Code, Procreate

The challenge

Blå Sol Festival wanted to encourage more sustainable behaviour among attendees. Many guests left cups and waste behind despite existing recycling initiatives. The challenge was to create a gamified digital solution that would motivate young adults to actively participate in waste collection and cup returns.

The goal

How can Blå Sol Festival motivate participants aged 18–25 to responsibly collect and dispose of waste through a gamified digital solution?

Discover Define Develop Deliver
Discover

Desk research, interviews, affinity diagram

We started with the discovering phase: desk research, 21 interviews with the target audience, plus conversations with people who have experience organising festivals. From the interview answers we built an affinity diagram to cluster what we heard.

Define

Conclusion and persona

After identifying key insights and patterns from our research, we created a persona to define our target user and better understand their needs, goals and motivations.

Develop

Wireframing and prototyping

After defining our target user, we developed and iterated on concepts through wireframing and prototyping — translating user needs into a functional and engaging solution. Early sketches and low-fidelity wireframes helped explore ideas quickly and shape the core user flow.

The style tile defined the visual direction, bringing together the colours, typography, imagery and overall mood.

Deliver

The final app

View the prototype

What we learned

Throughout this project, we learned how research-driven design can be used to address real-world challenges in a meaningful way. One of the key learnings was the importance of validating assumptions through research. While gamification initially appeared to be a suitable approach, our findings helped us understand how reward systems can influence user behaviour and encourage more responsible actions.

At the same time, we recognized the limitations of designing without access to a real festival environment. Future testing would be essential to evaluate the long-term effectiveness of the reward system and identify opportunities for further improvement.

Overall, this project strengthened our understanding of the UX design process and demonstrated how digital solutions can contribute to more sustainable and engaging experiences when they are built around genuine user and organizational needs.

An exam project for young coffee enthusiasts in Aarhus who want to discover high-quality cafés and connect with others who share the same passion.

PlaceAarhus, Denmark
ToolsFigma, VS Code, Procreate

Aarhus has a dense speciality-coffee scene, but newcomers have no easy way to tell which café fits the moment — the atmosphere, the price level, the quality, whether it is easy to get to. And drinking coffee is social, yet there was nowhere to find other people who care about it as much.

The goal

Define

Young coffee enthusiasts new to the city: curious, quality-driven, and looking for company as much as for a good cup.

Develop

Copenhagen Fashion Week · Prototyping, Multimedia Design

CPHFW

Client

Copenhagen Fashion Week

Role

UX Research, UI/UX Design, Prototyping

Duration

8 weeks

Date

November 2025

Tools

Figma, FigJam, VS Code

The challenge

“Dark and avant-garde.” “Colourful and grandma-like.” “Y2k-girly and flower power.” Our interviewees dressed nothing alike — but they all cared about sustainability, and they all wanted to know where to go. Fashion Week information is scattered, and going alone is intimidating.

The goal

Two connected platforms: an info screen at the venue for schedules, brands and sustainability facts, and a companion app on the phone for saving events, discounts and finding company. Our five values guided every decision — interactive, stylish, informative, clean, community.

Discover Define Develop Deliver
Discover

Field research and five interviews

We observed Fashion Week attendees on three premises - interest and inspiration, values around sustainability, and web behaviour - then ran five interviews to back our assumptions. Sustainability in fashion mattered to everyone we spoke to, and social media is where they look first. From that we built a persona: someone with a passion for fashion who shows their personality through their wardrobe, and who wants both information and company.

Define

From canvas to requirements

A Value Proposition Canvas showed how much information the solution had to carry — schedules, sustainable clothing, new brands. We ideated with the How Might We method, grouped the results into an affinity diagram, and turned them into a list of requirements that doubled as our task flow. Twelve candidate values went up on the wall; five got the most sticker votes and stayed.

Interactive Stylish Informative Clean Community
Develop

Low fidelity, on paper first

We split the work between info screen and companion app and mapped both out on paper in a brainstorm session. The info screen was meant to read like a fashion poster — a gallery first, lots of air between elements, bold typography. Testing the LOFI gave us confidence in the flow: the changes were small ones. More time on the QR code, a clearer signal that the screen is interactive, a scroll bar in the menu, and a proper back button.

Deliver

The high fidelity prototype

A style tile set the visual direction — typography, colour and mockups — with black and white leading and colour used sparingly in the app. The second round of usability tests ran from the info screen through to the app, and came back with a high success rate and only minor fixes: the navigation bar and the missing back buttons. The looping carousel in the companion app was coded with Swiper.
